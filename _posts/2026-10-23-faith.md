# Faith and consistency

This one's more of a braindump, and I might update this whenever I please. 

More often than not, I don't realize how slow the learning process truly is, especially when I either found a lot of my classes easy, or have simply forgotten how much of a challenge I put myself through, because at the day-to-day level, it wasn't particularly challenging. 

A lot of my best work has come out in bursts, and I'm slowly trying to learn how to *amortize* that. Rather than wanting to do a lot every day, start small and see where it goes. There are only really two things I need to maintain: a good sense of willpower, and faith in myself {% fn %}. 

I sense a lack of faith in the world these days. Quite an unsubstantiated statement, I know. To avoid beating a dead horse in 2026, I'll leave it at that. Most importantly, we need faith in others as much as we do ourselves.
